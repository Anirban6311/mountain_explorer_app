## 5. Edge Cases
1. **Guest signs in mid-session** → ChecklistPage watches `AuthCubit`; once authenticated, subscribe to new uid's stream.
2. **Item text > 120 chars** → reject with `ValidationError`.
3. **Empty submission** → ignore (no snackbar needed).
4. **Firestore permission-denied** → map to `PermissionError`; snackbar with "Sign in again." Bounce to /login.
